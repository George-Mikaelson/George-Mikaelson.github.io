How it works

You see the work before you spend anything, and you pay in two halves.

  1. I make a preview

    Free, built for your place from what is already public, like your map listing and menu. I send you the link.

  2. You decide

    If you like it, tell me what to change. If you don't, you owe nothing.

  3. 50% to start

    You pay half in crypto, and I make your changes and finish the work.

  4. 50% on delivery

    You pay the rest when the site is live or the video is ready, and the files are yours.
