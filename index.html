An editorial digital experience for a specialty coffee concept, combining minimalist art direction, product storytelling and a carefully structured menu experience.

Design highlights

  • The opening pours an iced latte as you scroll, rendered frame by frame in 3D.
  • Six drinks, one at a time: the menu changes the photograph as you read down the list.
  • One typeface family whose narrow and wide cuts carry the whole identity.
  • Mobile first: the pour, the menu and the bar locations all work on a phone.

Tell us about it

What we do

Six things, all of them for hospitality. Most projects use several.

  • Website design

    A design drawn from your room, your plates and your guests. Every page is laid out for the way people choose where to eat.

  • Web development

    Fast, responsive and technically sound. Pages that open quickly on a phone with weak signal outside your door, and that search engines can read.

  • Digital menu experience

    Menus that are a pleasure to browse and easy to keep up to date: readable on a phone, accessible, with prices, allergens and photos where they help.

  • Restaurant brand experience

    A digital identity that feels like an evening in your room: the light, the materials, the pace and the voice, carried consistently from homepage to booking.

  • Multi-location websites

    One system for a group or a chain: shared brand and menus, with a page for each location that carries its own address, hours and booking.

  • Conversion optimisation

    Structure that turns a visit into a booking, an order or a walk-in. The menu and the next step are always one tap away.

How a project runs

Five steps, in this order. You talk to the person who designs and builds your site at every one of them.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We get to know your restaurant, your brand, your guests and what the website has to achieve.

  2. 02

    Strategize

    We plan the structure of the site, the creative direction and how guests will move from first look to booking.

  3. 03

    Design

    We design a visual experience made for your brand, and you see it before a line of code is written.

  4. 04

    Develop

    We build the design into a responsive, fast website that works on every screen.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We publish, test on real phones and browsers, and refine the details once guests start using it.
