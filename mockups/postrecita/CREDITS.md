## Map and venue details

`img/map.webp` is made from OpenStreetMap tiles. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

Address, hours, phone, email and cuisine come from the public OpenStreetMap listing https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11717508832, read on 2026-11-18. Nothing else about the venue is claimed: no prices, dishes, reviews or awards.

OpenStreetMap has no street address for Postrecita; the area (Antiguo Cuscatlán) comes from the position of the map pin.
