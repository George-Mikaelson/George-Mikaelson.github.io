At the table

Thin, crisp, made here

The dough is made in our kitchen and stretched thin, so the crust stays crisp. Plenty of flavours, savoury and sweet. A few that guests keep mentioning:

Vegan and gluten-free options too. Ask us for tonight’s full list.

A cosy room lit by candles, and wines picked to go with the pizza. Good for a quiet dinner for two or a long table after a day out.
