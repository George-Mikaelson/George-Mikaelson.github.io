Two brothers, one idea

José and Kevin Martínez were born in Los Angeles to Salvadoran parents. In November 2023, after opening a hotel in San Miguel, they opened Azteca in San Salvador with one goal: Mexican food that tastes like Mexico, served with real care.

On the table
