How it works

  1. Assessment

    You explain your situation and Ian tells you which visa options fit and what they need.

  2. Documents

    You get a checklist. Ian reviews each document before anything is lodged.

  3. Lodgement

    The application is lodged with the Department of Home Affairs, and Ian handles their requests until a decision.

No migration agent can promise a visa. The Department of Home Affairs makes every decision.
