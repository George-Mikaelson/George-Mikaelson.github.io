Always verify the address

After you paste an address, compare the first and last characters with this page. Some malware swaps copied addresses, so look every time.

Pick the network that matches the address. Coins sent on another network, such as USDC on Ethereum or Base to the Solana address, will not arrive, and nobody can send them back. If you are unsure, send a small test first.

If an email or message ever gives you a different address, do not pay. Email me at the address below first.

Step by step

Pick the route that matches what you have. If this is your first crypto payment, send a small test amount, wait for my confirmation, then send the rest.

A. USDC from an exchange

Fewest new apps. Good if you will only pay once.

  1. Open an exchange account

    Coinbase, Kraken and Binance all sell USDC and can send it on Solana; others may too. They ask for an ID check, which can take minutes or a few days.

  2. Buy USDC

    Deposit money by bank transfer or card, then buy USDC. Buy a little more than the invoice to cover the withdrawal fee.

  3. Withdraw to my address

    Choose Send or Withdraw, pick USDC, set the network to Solana, paste the address and check the first and last four characters.

  4. Check the amount that arrives

    Some exchanges take their fee out of the amount you type. Make sure the invoiced amount is what arrives.

  5. Email me the transaction ID

    The exchange shows it as TxID, transaction hash or signature. Exchanges cannot read the invoice QR, so this is how I match your payment.

B. With a Solana wallet app

Scan the invoice QR and the payment is matched to your invoice by itself.

  1. Install Phantom or Solflare

    Get it from your phone's app store or the official site (phantom.com, solflare.com). Write the recovery phrase on paper and never share it. I will never ask for it, and neither will any real support team.

  2. Add USDC and a little SOL

    Withdraw USDC from an exchange to your wallet's Solana address, or use the app's Buy button (higher fees). Network fees are paid in SOL, and a dollar's worth is plenty.

  3. Scan the invoice QR

    Tap the scan icon in the app. My address, the amount, USDC and a payment reference fill in by themselves.

  4. Check, then confirm

    Check the first and last four characters of the address and the amount, then confirm. The transfer settles in seconds.

C. Bitcoin or ETH

For places that already hold BTC or ETH, from the till or a personal wallet.

  1. Use the amount on the invoice

    I work out the BTC or ETH amount from the dollar price on the day I send the invoice.

  2. Send to the matching address

    BTC goes to the bc1p address on the Bitcoin network, ETH to the 0x address on Ethereum. Paste it and check the first and last characters.

  3. Leave room for the fee

    Your wallet or exchange adds a network fee. Make sure the invoiced amount is what arrives.

  4. Email me the transaction ID

    Your wallet calls it the transaction ID or hash. I confirm once the network has confirmed it.

What is inside a Solana invoice QR

Invoices paid in USDC carry a Solana Pay link, shown as a QR code and as text. Your wallet reads it and fills in the payment. Here is an example for a 75 USDC deposit.

solana:7SJQyVwzmxLgM8SD9XAbMHwFgdPjphHapBZNnBXp7H46?amount=75&spl-token=EPjFWdd5AuFqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v&reference=(unique per invoice)&label=George%20Mikaelson&message=GM-2026-001%20deposit&memo=GM-2026-001-1

PartWhat it does
addressWhere the money goes. Always the Solana address above.
amountThe invoiced amount in USDC. The general QR on this page has none, so you type it.
spl-tokenThe USDC mint, so your wallet sends real USDC and not another token.
referenceA random public key made for this invoice only. It lets me find your payment on the public ledger. It holds no funds and nobody can sign with it.
label, messageShown in your wallet so you know who you are paying and for what. Not stored on the ledger.
memoThe invoice number, written on the public ledger with the payment. It never contains your name.

Exchanges do not read Solana Pay links. If you pay from an exchange, use the plain address and email me the transaction ID.

What happens after you pay

Every payment is public on its blockchain, so both of us can check it.

  1. I confirm it

    I check it on a public block explorer and reply by email, usually the same day.

  2. Work starts

    The delivery time in your quote counts from the confirmed deposit and the moment I have everything listed in the brief.

  3. You review

    A preview link or a draft of the video. One revision round is included before you pay anything more.

  4. Balance, then handover

    You pay the balance within 5 working days of approving the work. Then you get the files and the live site, plus a receipt by email.
